1. Project Objective
The objective was to design a daily and weekly productivity system that:

3. Version History (V1 → V2 → V3)
 • V1 (Minimalistic & Relaxed):
  ○ Advantages: Simple, easy to follow, low cognitive load.
  ○ Disadvantages: Too general, insufficient structure, lacked alignment with long-term goals.
  ○ Visual Summary: Light routine, no strict categories, no weekly structure, no job-search intensity model; good for recovery, not for high performance.
 • V2 (Structured & Time-Boxed):
  ○ Advantages: Highly modular, precise, professional, portfolio-ready.
  ○ Disadvantages: Too rigid, too many time blocks, high cognitive load, not aligned with real-world variability.
  ○ Visual Summary: Hour-by-hour schedule, fixed blocks for each category, strong discipline, but unsustainable long-term.
 • V3 (Agile, Modular, Flexible - Final Golden Standard):
  ○ Advantages: Perfect balance between structure and freedom, aligned with real cognitive rhythms, compatible with weekly objectives, sustainable and scalable, ideal for job-search + learning + well-being.
  ○ Disadvantages: Requires daily self-management (intentionality), relies on weekly planning discipline.
  ○ Visual Summary: No hours, only categories, Specific/General structure, daily flexibility, weekly backbone, modular, calm, powerful.
4. Reasoning Behind Choosing V3
AI helped evaluate the trade-offs between the three versions:
 • Why V1 was rejected: Insufficient structure, no weekly backbone, no integration of job-search requirements, too permissive for a high-stakes transition year.
 • Why V2 was rejected: Time-boxing created unnecessary pressure, rigidity reduced adaptability, high risk of burnout, not aligned with the user’s natural workflow.
 • Why V3 was chosen: Combines the clarity of V2 with the calmness of V1, supports daily variability, aligns with job-search intensity, integrates weekly objectives, modular and scalable, ideal for long-term consistency, psychologically sustainable.
